    A run collects what was disclosed since the previous run. Each item is dated by when its source first published it, which can be days or months after the event. Windows count back from the latest run's date, not from today, so a missed run does not empty the page.

    incident
    an AI model or agent acted outside its scope on real systems, or was exploited or used to run a real attack
    vuln
    a disclosed flaw or working exploit in an AI product, agent framework, MCP server, model-serving stack or AI dev tool
    capability
    measurements of models' offensive cyber ability, and of models gaming or recognizing evaluations
    evaluator
    other findings, methods, funding, deals and policy from the organizations that evaluate AI safety
    crit
    confirmed compromise of a real third party or production system by or through an AI system; actively exploited; CVSS 9.0 or higher
    high
    unsanctioned or destructive action on live systems; working exploit against a shipped AI product; CVSS 7.0–8.9; a large measured jump in offensive capability
    med
    CVSS 4.0–6.9; a new attack technique shown in research; routine capability results
    low
    CVSS below 4.0; minor issues
    info
    analysis, follow-ups, methods, funding, policy

    A vulnerability with a published CVSS score takes that score's band. Status says who stands behind an incident or vulnerability: confirmed means the affected vendor, operator or an official body confirmed it; reported means a researcher or the press reported it; disputed means the named party disputes it.

    Sources: the GitHub Advisory Database for AI-ecosystem packages, the AI Incident Database, security researchers' and vendors' disclosures, AI security institutes (UK AISI, US CAISI and others), evaluators' own publications, and major press. Every item links to the page it was taken from. Before a run is published, a separate checker re-opens its sources and rejects what they do not support.

    Records before October 10, 2026 were loaded in one batch from an earlier monitor; daily runs start on October 28, 2026. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
